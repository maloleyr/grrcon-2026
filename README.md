# grrcon-2026

My notes and observations from GrrCON 2026.

_These are my personal notes about my personal experience. These are not professional notes nor a full review of the conference. These are my personal opinions and should you be offended then that is on you. As the program clearly states: "Attend at your own risk of being offended, but also don't be a jerk."_

## tl;dr

What an amazing conference! Honestly I don't think there were any real missed or bad sessions this year. From the opening keynote to the final session I attended I think I learned, or had a prior though reinforced, a ton this year. Even better though was connecting with vendors and friends on a different level now that I have a role in running the #misec organization (a 501c3 nonprofit in Michigan). I was also able to chat with a few vendors regarding my professional role and what other services might help out. I'm looking forward to a followup with Cloudflare on that. I think the highlight was the Grand Rapids #misec Meet Up at The Bob on Thursday evening. I cannot tell you how many people were there for our meetup but I can tell you that many had expensive tastes (Knob Creek and Woodford Reserve and Ketel One?!?!?!).

## Wednesday

Wednesday evening I always try and hang around downtown for a quiet drink or three with some close friends that travel in for GrrCON. Unfortunately my usual place (Twisted Tap) is no longer open on Wednesday's and I totally forgot! Instead I stopped in at Cinco De Mayo for some tacos... which was not the best choice of the evening. I don't know what happened there but the service was very poor and the tacos were just so bland. I just expected more.

After tacos I pivoted to the unofficial GrrCON hangout: Z's Bar and Restaurant! Z's is always a good time on its own but being full of friends and soon to be friends made it extra special. I especially enjoyed talking to some presenters (Patrick and Tom and John and others) and reconnecting with friends that I only see but once a year. Ed and his 3D printer went all out and it was fun getting a preview.

My #misec President joined me on a trek _o'er the river and through the woods_ to meet with one of our #misec Elders, Brian, for a meet and greet with some sales people at the Drop Drop. Now THAT is a cocktail lounge and what a wonderful old fashioned it was. Of course the trek back to my car was long. I got my steps in!

I had to stop at Meijer for last minute #misec supplies and then I went to bed! Thursday would come soon enough.

Thursday is by far the longest day of the conference for me. I was downtown from 8am until nearly midnight.

#### Session Notes (Kind of raw and messy, be warned)

1. Keynote: GrrCON Awakening: How Humans Win
    - This was one of the best opening keynotes that I've heard in a while.
    - In classic GrrCON fashion it was full of expletives and real human thought and opinion.
    - Build community > corporate world/life. We are humans and we deserve to be around other humans and treated with respect.
    - Don't compromise on your values.
    - Listen. Support. Share the love.
    - Remember that the person next to you could be your future boss. Be kind to each other.
    - This was the phrase of the year: "Self licking ice cream cone of misery."
    - Cyber insurance: Go F### yourself. (to be clear I only agree in the professional sense that insurance should be way more proactive and they need to stop being jerks when an incident happens.)
    - On insurance: Especially F### the "War Clause" and terrorist clauses that always appear.
        - Seriously. On a personal note I get the _why_ but this just reduces humans to units. 
    - On insurance: The business model goal of trying to not pay is evil.
    - Rather have a blind proctologist rather than go get another certification.
        - I do not disagree here. Certs can be good if they enrich you personally _and_ professionally. 
    - "Give me a Google cert!" While this certainly got a chuckle it speaks to a real truth: I don't need someone with a certification - I need someone who knows how to find the answer when it counts. Whether they know it by memory or they can find it on a wiki. 
    - Certs == scam money grab for a captive industry.
    - Which is it? Unfilled jobs are numerous, so are the people looking for work, and yet corps are not hiring. This is pure marketing and these companies are not serious. 
    - VC rule of 40: 40% Year over Year. ----> PE Firm Logic.
    - Too much of this thought: The Return Must Be Greater Than "Is it good for Humans and Society?" 
        - I think it is clear that the people in this keynote are commenting on a lot more than just AI and society. I echo many of these thoughts. I do think that a business should not exist solely to make a greater return of money; it should also exist to benefit the human society that allows it to exist.
    - We have a fundamental right to operate in a safe place.
    - We don't have actual real "AI" right now. We have both real and natural stupidity at times. 
    - To the vendors and services people: The Enterprise market is picked clean! Focus on SMB and mom/pop shops. They need help!! 
        - This one speaks to me. The services I offer are not tailored to the enterprise. Can they work there? Yep! I'll gladly take enterprise budget. However I work with smaller orgs for a reason: They can't afford the enterprise prices. 
    - Do not purchase based solely on analyst (cough GARTNER) reports. These are pay-to-play.
    - Scarcity -> Specialization. The story of human society.
    - There exists a ceiling to thinking.
        - That one is a bit fuzzy in my notes and memory.

Overall a very excellent opening keynote and much better than last year.

    - Dave made the original (and still develops) the Social Engineering Toolkit (SET).
    - Hype or Revolution? It's both. Advantage goes to those that test claims and ship good systems.
    - Trust is earned, not given. Apply this to humans and AI.
    - AI Leaders are NOT security minded folks.
    - Check out the book: Build a Large Language Model.
    - JEB -> Fast classifier. 
    - Start small and ship something.
    - Nightwire: github/hackingdave/nightwire
    - NightBeacon
    - AI simplifies complexity.
        - This is so true! I cannot keep my codebase in my head and know all the functions. AI can and this is the biggest win for me.
    - Context beats clever prompts.
    - Magic Unicorn V2 coming soon.
        - I believe this is one of his projects.
    - AI Drafts -> Analyst human owns final call.

6. What Happens to Cybersecurity When The Internet Fails
    - I almost gave this a 'meh' grade _but_ the wisdom that Dick has was excellent. Yeah, he's an older man and yes the microphone was a challenge and yes he didn't get through the entire presentation (ran out of time) STILL it was very interesting to learn some history.
    - The talk was supposed to discuss security post and pre internet. We got the pre-internet talk of his time at Ma Bell.
    - Hacker since 1961. One of the first coders after spending time as a linesman and other tasks... 
        - The whole "other duties as assigned" carried a lot of water back then I guess. 
    - Free internet is an artifact of the Great Depression and the New Deal REA.
    - Epistemic Trespassing. 
    - Acronym vs Initialism.
    - Phantom pooper was a wild story. Phone company had a mystery pooper who pooped in the payphone booth. Dick was assigned to investigate. He did a literal stake out and caught the child doing the pooping. Dragged the child back home.
        - OK so I have a ton of personal thoughts here. Knowing it was a different time and society has changed a ton.
        - I cannot imagine the modern outrage for a strange man dragging a strange child home to his stay at home mother. I also cannot imagine the local response if the strange man was a man of color. 
        - Just things to think about...
    - Car phone in 1955 story. The customer was a completely vile person when talking to the phone operators. So Ma Bell decided to revoke his car phone. Dick had to go into the (private) vehicle and dismantle the phone while other Ma Bell employees stood guard and kept the customer away from his vehicle.
        - Again - a ton of thoughts here. 
        - One: We had car phones in 1955??
        - Two: Umm this is a private personal vehicle, yes? 
        - Three: Licensing agreements haven't changed in ever, eh? Did we ever have rights? Or is living simply a privilege that can be revoked? 
    - Background (photo to link)
    - Strowger Step-by-Step Switch.
    - 1960s: Death of competence and loyalty (from company to people).

### \#misec Meet Up

This was the second annual Grand Rapids #misec Meet Up at GrrCON! First, I feel so blessed and honored to be in a position and a time in my life where I can help bring such a group of people together. Second, this group is FUN! And third: I heard we put on a better time than some other people that shall not be named... 

So this is a simple event: It's not hosted; it's not sponsored; and it's open to any security professional based in Michigan. In particular this is for our in-town #misec people to meet and greet with other #misec people throughout Michigan. There are no presentation or sales pitches. It's literally just a social meet up with other like-minded people.

We will do this again next year so look for more information as we approach GrrCON 2027! This is also a preamble to MISECCON2027.
